Five discrete pips rather than a progress bar, because the track has exactly five steps. The dashed pip is the one you can buy next, and its cost sits on the row against what you actually hold — the only number a player is trying to work out.

Only active knights defend Catan. A knight that acts this turn is deactivated; 1 wheat brings it back.

Every disabled button says its own condition ("needs politics 3", "needs 1 brick"). Rank, active and acted are three independent facts, so each gets a word — "active but has already acted" cannot be read off two shades of the same colour.
