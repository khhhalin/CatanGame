Notices, banners & errors

Four levels of interruption, from a field error to a page-wide connection bar. None of them is a modal — an error modal in a real-time game hides the board it is talking about.

1 · inline field errors

Marek is already at this table. Pick another name.
2–20 characters. Everyone at the table sees it.

aria-invalid plus aria-describedby pointing at the message: a screen reader then reads the error when focus lands on the field, which a red border alone never does. The message names the value that is wrong instead of saying "invalid input".

2 · toast notices

✓
Trade accepted

Tomasz took your 1 ore for 2 wood.

i
Your turn

Roll the dice within 15 seconds or the turn passes.

!
Bank is out of ore

Nobody collects ore until some is paid back in.

!
Cannot build there

A settlement must be at least two roads from the nearest other settlement. Ewa's is one road away.

Only the failure sits in role="alert". The routine three share one role="status" region, so a screen reader is not interrupted by every dice roll. The error does not auto-dismiss — the life bar is absent — because a rejection the player did not read is a rejection they will hit again. The message names the actual rule and the actual blocker, not "invalid move".

3 · section banners

A game is running. House rules are fixed until it ends.
Waiting for one more player. Kalina set the minimum to 3.
Cities & Knights is on. The game ends at 13 victory points, not 10.

A standing condition of one panel, living inside that panel. It has no dismiss — the condition is still true after you close it, so a dismiss button would be a lie.

4 · page-level bars

Reconnecting… Your last move is queued. The board may be out of date.
Disconnected. The Socket.IO library could not be loaded, so the game cannot connect. Check your network and reload.
Back online. Reloaded the board from the server — you are up to date.

The bar takes its own layout row rather than overlaying the shell, so it never covers the board a worried player is staring at. Only the reconnecting one animates, and its pulse is switched off under prefers-reduced-motion.
