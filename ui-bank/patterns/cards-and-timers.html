An offer you made shows every player's answer as it arrives, so you are not guessing whether anyone has seen it. Incoming offers put the ring next to the Accept button — the countdown and the thing it constrains belong together.

Countdown treatments

Colour follows the same three steps everywhere: neutral, --warn under ~40%, --bad under ~15%. The seconds are always spelled out, so the colour is a reinforcement and never the message.
